# Proposal

## Purpose

Check that a commercial proposal tells the client clearly what they get, by when and for how much, and that the author can keep every promise in it.

## What to check

- Scope: the client can tell exactly what is delivered and what is not.
- Price: every amount is consistent across the summary, tables and payment terms. Totals add up. Currency and VAT treatment are stated.
- Timeline: dates are realistic. Dates that depend on the client (feedback, access, data, approvals) are stated as such.
- Promises: no guarantee of an outcome the author does not control (results, rankings, uptime of third parties, legal outcomes).
- Terms: payment terms, validity of the offer, and what happens on changes of scope.
- The ask: the reader knows what to do next to accept.

## Out of scope

- Visual design and formatting preferences.
- Word choice, unless it changes meaning or misleads.

## Impact anchors

- critical: wrong price, a legal commitment the author did not intend, wrong client, leaked confidential data.
- high: a wrong fact or misleading claim the client will act on (dates, scope, deliverables).
- medium: an unclear ask, a missing term, or the wrong tone for the audience.
- low: style.
