# Standard operating procedure

## Purpose

Check an SOP before people (or agents) use it: someone who has not done the task before can follow it start to finish and get the same result, safely.

## What to check

- Scope: when to use this procedure, when not to, and who owns it.
- Inputs: what you need before starting (access, tools, data, approvals).
- Steps: in order, one action each, with the exact place to click or command to run. No step depends on knowledge that is not written down.
- Decisions: every branch says what to check and what to do in each case.
- Outputs: what "done" looks like, and how to verify it.
- Failure: what to do when a step fails, and who to escalate to.
- Safety: steps that delete, send, pay or publish are marked, with a check before them.
- Currency: tool names, menu paths and links match the current versions; the document has an owner and a review date.

## Out of scope

- Formatting preferences.
- Improvements to the process itself, unless the written process cannot work.

## Impact anchors

- critical: a step that can delete data, send to the wrong people, move money or expose confidential data without a check.
- high: a step that cannot be followed as written, a missing decision branch, a wrong command or path.
- medium: missing inputs or verification, no escalation path.
- low: wording.
