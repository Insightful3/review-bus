# Plan

## Purpose

Check a plan (project plan, launch plan, technical plan, strategy) before work starts: it reaches its goal, the steps are in a workable order, and the risks are visible.

## What to check

- Goal: what success looks like, measurably, and by when.
- Scope: what is in and out; nothing important is assumed without being written.
- Steps: in an order that works; dependencies between steps and on other people are named.
- Time and effort: estimates are realistic for the people and budget available; the critical path is clear.
- Risks: the biggest risks are named with what would trigger them and what to do then.
- Assumptions: the plan's key assumptions are stated, and the plan says how to check them early.
- Decisions: open decisions are listed with who decides and by when.
- Ownership: every step has one owner.

## Out of scope

- Formatting and document structure.
- A different strategy, unless the plan cannot reach its own goal.

## Impact anchors

- critical: the plan cannot reach its goal as written, or commits money or people that are not available.
- high: a missing dependency or risk that will likely delay the plan, an unrealistic estimate on the critical path.
- medium: missing owners, unclear success measures, unstated assumptions.
- low: wording.
